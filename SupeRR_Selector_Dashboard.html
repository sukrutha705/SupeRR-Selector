How the shortlist was built

MODEL SUMMARY
1. Define pressure: combine innings progress, wickets lost, chase-rate stress and resource scarcity. A delivery is pressure-qualified at index ≥ 0.55.
2. Compare with self: score pressure output against each player's non-pressure baseline, not just against other players.
3. Apply reliability gates: minimum pressure volume, innings/spells, baseline sample and recent appearances; shrink relative ratios for low samples.
4. Rank by role: separate batting and bowling composites, then verify each name against the supplied player mapping and role-specific delivery records.

Transparent, role-specific scoring

Pressure is a match-context property, not a label attached to a player. Each player's pressure performance is compared with their own ordinary-situation baseline.

Threshold ≥ 0.55

Pressure index construction

HEURISTIC MODEL
FIRST INNINGS

Progress + wickets

0.55 × innings progress + 0.45 × wickets-lost fraction. Later stages and lost wickets raise pressure.

SECOND INNINGS

Chase stress + resources

0.45 × chase-rate stress + 0.30 × resource scarcity + 0.25 × wickets-lost fraction.

CLASSIFICATION

Pressure delivery

Deliveries with pressure index ≥ 0.55 are included in pressure performance calculations.

Batting composite

35% absolute pressure strike rate percentile
15% absolute dismissal-avoidance percentile
35% shrunk improvement in strike rate vs own baseline
15% shrunk improvement in dismissal avoidance

Bowling composite

30% absolute pressure economy percentile (lower is better)
20% pressure wicket-rate percentile
30% shrunk economy improvement vs own baseline
20% shrunk wicket-rate improvement

Fairness, scope & safeguards

DATA SCOPE

IPL + T20I, 2022 onward

Only IPL competitions and T20I-format competitions included. Other domestic T20 competitions excluded.

ELIGIBILITY

Minimum evidence

At least 100 pressure legal balls, 5 pressure innings/spells, baseline volume (30 batting / 60 bowling legal balls) and 3 appearances in 2025–26.

ADJUSTMENTS

Context-aware handling

Recent seasons weighted more; IPL weight 1.0, T20I weight 0.75. Relative ratios shrunk toward neutral by pressure-ball sample size.

Scoring is a transparent heuristic, not a claim of causal pressure effects. Bowling runs exclude byes/leg-byes; illegal wides/no-balls are excluded from legal-ball denominators; selected non-bowler dismissals are excluded from bowler wickets. Innings 3 and 4 are excluded.

Sensitivity & limitations

The original analysis compared pressure thresholds 0.50, 0.55 and 0.60, and IPL-only vs IPL+T20I rankings. The top-10 overlap varied, so rankings should be treated as a model-based shortlist rather than absolute truth. Small samples and competition transfer remain uncertainties; current auction availability, retention status, fitness and price are outside the supplied data.

How to interpret the squad-fit notes

The individual role suggestions are hypotheses based on the observed pressure profile (for example, top-order tempo, middle-order acceleration, spin control or pace depth). They are not claims that RR currently have a specific vacancy, that a player will be in the 2027 auction, or that the player is available at a feasible price. RR should cross-check shortlist profiles against the confirmed retained squad, purse, overseas limits, player registration, fitness and auction pool before making bids.
